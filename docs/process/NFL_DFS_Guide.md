# NFL DFS — Guide & Cheat Sheet

NFL DFS is the most popular format and the most correlated. One player's big game is almost always tied to someone else's: a QB's 350 yards are his WR's 150. If you learn only one thing, learn **stacking**. If you learn two, learn to **read Vegas**.

---

## 1. The Basics

### Formats
| Format | Goal | Build style |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash** (50/50s, double-ups, H2H) | Beat ~half the field | Volume, floors, safe usage, minimal correlation risk |
| **GPP / Tournaments** | Beat almost everyone | Stacks, game theory, leverage, ceiling over floor |
| **Showdown / Single game** | One game, captain 1.5x | Heavy correlation; captain choice is everything |

### Roster (DraftKings classic)
- 1 QB, 2 RB, 3 WR, 1 TE, 1 FLEX (RB/WR/TE), 1 DST — $50,000
- FanDuel: same structure, $60,000, half-PPR

### Scoring you actually need to know (DK)
- Pass yards 0.04/yd (25 yds = 1 pt) · Pass TD = 4 · INT = −1
- Rush/rec yards 0.1/yd · Rush/rec TD = 6 · **Reception = 1 (full PPR)**
- **Bonuses:** 300 pass yds = +3 · 100 rush yds = +3 · 100 rec yds = +3
- Fumble lost = −1 · 2-pt conversion = 2
- DST: sack 1 · INT/FR 2 · TD 6 · safety 2 · points allowed: 0 = 10, 1–6 = 7, 7–13 = 4, 14–20 = 1, 21–27 = 0, 28–34 = −1, 35+ = −4

**What this means:**
- **Full PPR makes targets the most valuable stat in the game.** A 6-catch, 50-yard day = 11 points before a TD.
- Pass-catching RBs are the best value in DFS. A 15-carry / 5-target back has two ways to score.
- Yardage bonuses reward ceiling. 100-yard games are worth 13+ before TDs.
- Rushing QBs are cheat codes — rushing yards at 0.1 vs. passing at 0.04.

## 2. Vegas — Start Here Every Week

Sportsbooks price every game with millions of dollars behind them. Use their work.

- **Game total** (over/under): 48+ = target both offenses. 40 or under = mostly avoid.
- **Implied team total** = (total ± spread) ÷ 2. Teams implied for 26+ points are where TDs live.
- **Spread** tells you *game script*:
  - Big favorite (−7+) → run-heavy second half → RB1 volume, DST upside
  - Big underdog → passing volume, garbage-time WR targets, QB attempts
  - Close spread (within 3) in a high total → the shootout you want to stack
- **Line movement** during the week signals injury news or sharp money. Recheck Sunday morning.

## 3. Position-by-Position

### Quarterback
The single most important correlated piece. Never play a QB alone in GPP.
- **Pass attempts** — 35+ projected is the volume you want. Look at pace and pass rate over expectation.
- **Rushing upside** — a QB who runs for 40 yards and a TD adds 10 points. Prioritize mobile QBs at similar prices.
- **Opponent** — pass defense ranked by EPA/play and DVOA, not yards allowed. Yards allowed is misleading (good teams face garbage time).
- **Implied total** 24+ and a game total 47+ is the sweet spot.
- Cash: high-floor, high-attempt QB in a good matchup. GPP: the QB with the best stack partners.

### Running Back
Volume is everything. Talent is second.
- **Opportunities** = carries + targets. 18+ is bell-cow territory. 22+ is elite.
- **Targets** — a back with 4+ targets/game has a PPR floor even in a bad rushing day.
- **Red zone / goal-line role** — who gets the carry from the 2? That's the TD equity.
- **Game script** — favorites' RBs get late-game volume. Underdog RBs need to catch passes.
- **Committee alert:** a 50/50 split kills DFS value regardless of talent.
- **Injury value:** when the starter is out, the backup at $4,500 with 20 touches is the best play on the slate. Every week has 1–3 of these. Find them.
- **Matchup:** rush defense by EPA and yards before contact allowed; also check pass-catching allowed to RBs.

### Wide Receiver
- **Target share** (25%+ = alpha) and **air yards share** — the two stats that predict WR production best.
- **Targets per game** — 8+ is a strong floor in PPR; 10+ is a WR1.
- **Snap share** 85%+ — he's on the field for every drop-back.
- **Coverage matchup** — slot vs. outside, man vs. zone. Some defenses funnel targets to the slot.
- **Cheap WRs** — target share spikes when a teammate is injured; that's where sub-$5,000 value lives.
- **Deep threats** are GPP-only: low floor, huge ceiling, low ownership.

### Tight End
The worst position. Don't overthink it.
- Either **pay up** for one of the 3–4 true target hogs, or **punt** with a $2,500–3,500 TE who has red-zone usage and spend the savings elsewhere.
- The mid-range ($4,000–5,500) TE tier is where value goes to die.
- Target share matters more than yards; TE TDs are essentially random outside the top few.

### Defense / Special Teams
- **Opponent** matters more than the DST itself. Target: bad offensive lines, turnover-prone QBs, backup QBs, low implied totals.
- **Vegas:** opponent implied 17 or under is the target; big home favorites are best.
- **Sack rate + turnover rate** of the defense; pressure rate of the D-line.
- Cheap DSTs in good spots regularly outscore expensive ones. Don't pay more than $3,500 unless the matchup is elite.
- Weather (wind/rain) boosts DST — more fumbles, fewer points.

## 4. Stacking — How Tournaments Are Won

### The core stack: QB + pass catcher(s)
When your QB throws 4 TDs, your WR probably caught 2 of them. You get paid twice for one event.
- **QB + WR1** is the baseline.
- **QB + WR1 + WR2/TE** (double stack) raises ceiling further.
- Stack the **target leader**, not necessarily the most expensive receiver.

### The bring-back (run-back)
Add a pass catcher from the **opposing** team. Shootouts go both ways — if your QB throws 4 TDs, the other team was probably scoring too.
- QB + WR + opposing WR = the classic GPP game stack.
- Best in games with totals 48+ and close spreads.

### Other correlations
- **RB + DST** from the same team (favored team runs the clock, defense gets sacks/turnovers)
- **Avoid negative correlation:** QB + opposing DST, RB + his own team's WR3, etc.
- **Don't** stack a QB with his own RB unless the RB is a heavy pass catcher.

### Cash-game correlation
Keep it light: one QB + one WR is fine. Skip the bring-back; you want floor, not ceiling.

## 5. Ownership & Leverage (GPP only)

- **Chalk** (25%+ owned) is usually the injury-replacement RB and the cheapest value plays. Everyone finds them.
- You can play some chalk, but **build around at least one contrarian stack**.
- **Leverage plays:** the second-best WR on a high-owned team, the RB in a game everyone expects to be a blowout, the QB in a high-total game that isn't the "obvious" one.
- Ask: *"If this game hits 55 points, which lineup type wins — and is that mine?"*
- Multi-entry: vary your QB stacks across 3–5 different games rather than 20 versions of one.
- **Late-swap** (DK): if your early-game player busts, swap late-game players for leverage. Structure lineups so key GPP pieces are in later windows.

## 6. Weekly Process

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| **Tue–Wed** | Review last week's snap counts, target shares, opportunity shares. Note injury reports. |
| **Thu** | Vegas lines settled. Identify 3–4 target games and the value RBs. |
| **Fri** | Practice reports (DNP / Limited / Full). Weather forecasts for outdoor games. |
| **Sat** | Build core lineups. Check ownership projections. |
| **Sun AM** | Inactives (90 min before kickoff). Final swaps. **Never** lock a lineup before inactives. |

### Injury report decoder
- **Out** — obvious
- **Doubtful** — assume out
- **Questionable** — 50/50; needs Sunday-morning confirmation; risky in cash
- **DNP Wed, Limited Thu, Full Fri** — usually plays
- **Limited all week** — often plays but with reduced role; avoid in cash

### Weather
- **Wind 15+ mph** — kills passing and kicking. Fade QBs/WRs, boost RBs and DSTs.
- **Rain** — mild effect, more fumbles.
- **Cold** alone is overrated. Wind is what matters.
- Domes: Atlanta, Detroit, Minnesota, New Orleans, Las Vegas, LA, Arizona, Indianapolis, Houston, Dallas.

## 7. Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why it hurts |
|---|---|
| Using yards allowed as matchup data | Bad teams face garbage time; use EPA/DVOA |
| Playing a QB without a stack partner | You're giving up the biggest correlation edge in the sport |
| Ignoring game script | RBs on 10-point underdogs get abandoned in the 2nd half |
| Overpaying for TE | Top 3 or punt; the middle is a trap |
| Locking before inactives | An inactive player = 0 points |
| Chasing last week's TDs | TDs are the noisiest stat; chase targets and carries |
| Building 20 lineups off one stack | If the game goes 17–13 you lose all 20 |
| Fading value RBs on principle | The $4,500 back with 20 touches is the best play, even at 40% owned. Play him and get leverage elsewhere. |

## 8. Pre-Lock Checklist

- [ ] Inactives checked for every player (posted 90 min before kickoff)
- [ ] Vegas totals & spreads rechecked Sunday morning
- [ ] Weather (wind above all) for outdoor games
- [ ] QB is stacked with at least one pass catcher
- [ ] GPP: bring-back from the opposing team
- [ ] No negatively correlated pieces (QB vs. own DST, etc.)
- [ ] RBs have 15+ projected opportunities (cash) or a clear ceiling path (GPP)
- [ ] TE is either top-tier or a punt — not the middle
- [ ] DST opponent implied 20 or under
- [ ] Salary within $300 of the cap
- [ ] Late-swap plan: are my leverage pieces in later games?

## ONE-PAGE CHEAT SHEET

**Slate order of operations:**
1. Vegas — rank games by total, teams by implied total.
2. Find the injury-driven value RBs (this defines the salary structure).
3. Pick 2–3 target games (48+ total, close spread).
4. Build QB + WR stack (+ bring-back for GPP).
5. Fill RB with volume, WR with targets, TE top-tier or punt, DST vs. bad offense.
6. Check ownership; add one contrarian angle.
7. Wait for inactives. Confirm. Lock.

**Targets by position:**
- **QB:** 35+ attempts · implied total 24+ · rushing upside · game total 47+
- **RB:** 18+ opportunities · 4+ targets · goal-line role · favored or pass-catching
- **WR:** 25%+ target share · 8+ targets · 85%+ snaps · air yards share 30%+
- **TE:** 7+ targets or red-zone role; otherwise punt
- **DST:** opp implied 17 or under · bad O-line · backup/turnover-prone QB · home favorite

**Correlation map:**
- ✅ QB + WR/TE · QB + WR + opp WR · RB + own DST · WR + WR (same team, high total)
- ❌ QB + opp DST · RB + own WR3 · two RBs from the same backfield

**Cash:** floors, volume, one light stack, no questionable tags, no wind games.
**GPP:** double stack + bring-back, one contrarian game, leverage vs. chalk, late-swap exposure.

**DK point math:** 100 rush yds + TD = 19 · 8 rec / 100 yds = 21 · 300 pass yds / 3 TD = 27 · QB 40 rush yds = +4
