# NBA DFS — Guide & Cheat Sheet

NBA is the most *predictable* DFS sport and the most *news-driven*. A player's production is almost entirely a function of **minutes × usage**, and both of those change nightly with injuries and rest. The edge isn't finding the best player — it's being awake at 6:55 PM when the news drops.

---

## 1. The Basics

### Formats
| Format | Goal | Build style |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash** (50/50s, double-ups, H2H) | Beat ~half the field | Minutes-secure starters, value plays with locked roles, high floors |
| **GPP / Tournaments** | Beat almost everyone | Ceiling, leverage on chalk, late-swap, blowout-proof or blowout-exploiting |
| **Showdown / Single game** | One game, captain 1.5x | Captain the usage king; correlate with pace |

### Roster (DraftKings classic)
- 1 PG, 1 SG, 1 SF, 1 PF, 1 C, 1 G, 1 F, 1 UTIL — $50,000
- FanDuel: 2 PG, 2 SG, 2 SF, 2 PF, 1 C — $60,000

### Scoring you actually need to know (DK)
- Point = 1 · 3PM = +0.5 bonus · Rebound = 1.25 · Assist = 1.5 · Steal = 2 · Block = 2 · Turnover = −0.5
- **Double-double = +1.5 · Triple-double = +3**

**What this means:**
- **Stat-stuffers beat scorers.** A 20/10/8 line (57.5 + 1.5 DD bonus) beats 30 points on 25 shots.
- Assists (1.5) and rebounds (1.25) are worth more than points. Point guards and bigs are the DFS engines.
- Steals and blocks (2 each) are high-variance — nice, but don't build around them.
- The triple-double bonus makes elite playmakers (Jokić, Dončić, Haliburton types) worth their price tag nearly every night.

## 2. Minutes — The Only Stat That Truly Matters

Fantasy points per minute is fairly stable. **Minutes are not.** Predict minutes, and you predict the slate.

### What determines minutes
- **Injuries** — the #1 factor. When a 34-mpg starter sits, 34 minutes get redistributed. Find who gets them.
- **Blowout risk** — starters sit the 4th quarter in 20-point games. Check the spread: 10+ point spreads are risky for stars, great for bench guys.
- **Back-to-backs** — some coaches limit minutes on the second night; stars sometimes rest entirely. Check the schedule.
- **Foul trouble** — random but real; bigs are more exposed.
- **Rotation trends** — a coach's recent 5 games tell you more than his season averages. Rotations shift constantly.

### The value-play equation
When a starter is ruled out:
1. Who starts in his place? (Usually a modest bump.)
2. **Who absorbs the usage?** Often a different player than the one who starts.
3. Which bench guy goes from 15 to 28 minutes at $3,500? **This is the play.**

A $3,500 player with 28 minutes and 20%+ usage is worth ~30 DK points = 8.5x value. That single play lets you afford two studs.

## 3. The Stats That Predict Production

| Stat | What it tells you | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| **Minutes (projected)** | Opportunity | 30+ for studs, 24+ for value |
| **Usage rate (USG%)** | Share of possessions used | 25%+ = go-to guy; watch for spikes with teammates out |
| **DK points / minute** | Efficiency | 1.0+ is good, 1.3+ elite |
| **Pace** | Possessions per game | Both teams fast = more stats for everyone |
| **Defensive rating (opponent)** | Matchup | Bottom-10 D = target |
| **Rebound rate / assist rate** | Stat-stuffing potential | Guys who fill multiple columns |
| **Position vs. defense (DvP)** | Matchup by position | Useful but overrated; pace and minutes matter more |

### Vegas for NBA
- **Game total** 230+ = fast, high-scoring, more stats.
- **Spread** within 6 = starters play full minutes. 10+ = blowout risk.
- **Implied team total** 118+ = your studs' team is scoring.

## 4. Value & Salary Structure

Every slate has a shape. Find the value first, then the studs fit.

### Stars & scrubs
The dominant NBA build. Two or three elite studs ($10,000+) with 30-minute floors and triple-double ceilings, funded by 2–3 injury-driven value plays under $4,500.
- Works because top-end NBA players are more reliable than in any other sport.
- Fails when the value plays' roles don't materialize — which is why news timing matters.

### Balanced
Mostly $6,000–8,500 players with secure roles. Safer in cash on slates with no clear value.

### The rule
- **Cash:** play the obvious value if the role is *confirmed* (starter ruled out, coach quote, starting lineup posted). Never guess in cash.
- **GPP:** the same value plays will be 40–60% owned. Play them, but get leverage with your studs and mid-tier.

## 5. News & Late Swap — Where the Edge Lives

NBA injury news breaks at 6:30–7:00 PM ET nightly, and the slate locks around 7:00 or 7:30. Whoever reacts fastest wins.

### Timeline
- **Afternoon:** initial injury report; questionable tags everywhere.
- **~90 min before tip:** teams post starting lineups. Confirms who starts, not who plays 30.
- **~30–60 min before tip:** "game-time decision" resolutions. This is when the biggest value plays are revealed.
- **After lock:** late games still have news. Late swap is your second chance.

### Late-swap strategy (DK)
- Put your **uncertain plays in later games** so you can swap them out if news changes.
- Keep your **safe studs in early games** — they're locked, but they're safe.
- If an early stud busts (injury in the 1st quarter), swap late-game players to raise ceiling and try to recover.
- A questionable star on a 10:30 PM game can be a leverage play — if he's ruled out after early lock, you swap; most people can't.

### Sources
- Team beat writers on X/Twitter (fastest)
- NBA official injury report (posted 5 PM ET, updated hourly)
- DFS news aggregators for consolidated feeds

## 6. Ownership & Leverage (GPP only)

- The injury-driven **value plays are chalk** and usually correct. Don't fade them out of principle.
- Leverage comes from **studs**: the $11,000 player at 15% instead of the $10,500 one at 45%.
- **Blowout-proof leverage:** the star in a close game while the field is on stars in a 12-point spread.
- **Second-tier usage:** when a star is out, everyone plays the obvious replacement. The *third* guy who gets 5 extra shots is often the better GPP play.
- Ask: *"What happens to the field if this value play flops?"* Structuring around that outcome is how you take down large fields.

## 7. Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why it hurts |
|---|---|
| Using season averages | Rotations and roles change weekly; use last 5–10 games |
| Ignoring blowout risk | Your $11,000 star sits the 4th quarter with 30 points instead of 50 |
| Playing a "questionable" player in cash | If he's out after lock, you have a zero |
| Overvaluing DvP matchups | Minutes and pace dwarf matchup in NBA |
| Chasing points instead of stat-stuffing | 30 points on 28 shots ≈ 34 DK; 15/12/9 ≈ 46 DK |
| Not using late swap | You're leaving your best tool in the box |
| Playing the 6th man over the starter | Starters get 30+; bench guys' minutes are volatile |
| Building before lineups post | Half your value plays might not have the role you assumed |

## 8. Pre-Lock Checklist

- [ ] Injury report checked within 30 min of lock
- [ ] Starting lineups confirmed for all your players
- [ ] No "questionable" or "GTD" players in cash lineups
- [ ] Every value play has a confirmed minutes reason (starter out, role change)
- [ ] Studs are in games with spreads under 10 (or I accept the blowout risk with intent)
- [ ] Back-to-back rest risk checked for stars
- [ ] Pace/total checked — no $9,000+ player in a 205-total slog
- [ ] Late-swap structure: uncertain pieces in later games
- [ ] Ownership reviewed — leverage in the stud/mid tier
- [ ] Salary within $200 of the cap

## ONE-PAGE CHEAT SHEET

**Slate order of operations:**
1. Injury report → who's out → who gets the minutes and usage.
2. Identify the value plays (cheap + 24+ minutes + role).
3. Vegas: totals 230+, spreads under 8 for studs.
4. Pick 2–3 studs with high DK/min and triple-double upside.
5. Fill mid-tier with minutes-secure starters in fast games.
6. Wait for starting lineups. Confirm. Lock.
7. Late swap as news breaks.

**Targets:**
- **Studs:** 34+ mpg · USG 28%+ · DK/min 1.3+ · spread under 8 · not on a B2B
- **Mid-tier:** 30+ mpg starters · fast game · multi-category stats
- **Value:** under $4,500 · 24+ projected minutes · confirmed role · USG bump

**Build guides:**
- **Cash:** 2 studs + 2 confirmed values + 4 secure 30-min starters. No GTDs. Avoid 12+ spreads.
- **GPP:** stars & scrubs, leverage on studs, one contrarian value, uncertain pieces in late games.

**Minutes math:** 34 min × 1.3 DK/min = 44 · 28 min × 1.1 = 31 (great for $4,000) · 20 min × 1.2 = 24 (not enough)

**Value formula:** target 5x salary ÷ 1,000 (e.g., $6,000 → 30 pts). 6x+ wins GPPs.

**Blowout rule:** spread 10+ → fade stars, target benches. Spread under 5 → starters play 36.

**News timing (ET):** 5:00 official report · ~5:30–6:00 lineups for 7:00 tips · 6:30–7:00 GTD decisions · late swap after.
